Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Temporary Email Generator ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, user accounts, or persistent identifiers. The temporary email addresses generated in the app are randomized and non-personal.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app fetches temporary mailbox data using the public 1secmail API. We do not transmit any private user data to external servers beyond requesting temporary emails from this service.

3. Data Retention

All emails received through temporary addresses are automatically deleted by the underlying email service according to their standard retention policies. No data is stored locally on your device long-term.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out via our official GitHub repository support page.
